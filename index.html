We are a Kenyan agribusiness that produces, aggregates and supplies premium processing potatoes — Markies, Manitou and Java — to leading processors. We also run a contract farming programme, provide agronomic advisory, crop monitoring, quality assurance and grading, logistics coordination, and the supply of agro-inputs to farmers.
We specialise in premium processing varieties — Markies, Manitou and Java. These are the varieties preferred by frozen chips and crisps manufacturers for their dry matter content, tuber size and consistency. Every batch is graded to processor specifications before delivery.
We identify suitable farmers, sign production agreements, and provide technical production guidelines, certified seed and agro-inputs. Our field officers conduct regular visits and monitor crop performance throughout the season, and we purchase the potatoes upon harvest at an agreed price. It gives farmers a guaranteed market and lets us scale supply without owning all the land. Contact us at sales@covercloudlimited.com to join.
Every batch undergoes inspection for variety purity, tuber size, dry matter content, maturity, freedom from disease, and minimal mechanical damage. Because produce comes from professionally managed farms with production data on record, we offer end-to-end traceability from farm to processor.
Yes. By combining our own commercial farms with a network of contract farmers staggered across different regions and growing seasons, we maintain a consistent, year-round supply of processing-grade potatoes — reducing procurement risk for processors.
Our agronomists provide soil fertility recommendations, fertiliser programmes, pest and disease management, irrigation guidance, harvest timing advice, and post-harvest handling training. The goal is simple: help every farmer achieve processor-grade quality and better profitability through modern farming practices.
Yes. We supply certified seed, fertilisers and crop protection products (agro-inputs) to our contracted farmers and other commercial growers, alongside agronomy and consultancy services. We're a reliable long-term partner — most farmers rejoin the programme season after season.
